# Recording prompts — at least 6 minutes, unscripted, camera on

These are rehearsal prompts, not words to read aloud. The task assesses the applicant's explanation and body language. Record your own voice and face with the screen visible. Aim for **6:30–7:00**, and check the actual media duration before uploading. There is no upper limit in the supplied submission text.

## Suggested walkthrough

| Approximate time | Show and explain in your own words |
|---|---|
| 0:00–0:50 | Introduce the caller problem and what the task readings changed about your design. Use aggregate observations; do not publish the transcript archive. |
| 0:50–3:30 | Pick a scenario from a supplied transcript privately. Select a fictional demo lead, start a call, move through stages, tap what happened, capture the prospect's need and record the next step. Explain an early exit and how the guide covers all eight stages. |
| 3:30–4:45 | Choose a deliberately inconsistent draft outcome, run Mistral debrief **before saving**, inspect its evidence-based suggestions, and choose whether to apply them. Explain why a post-call audit was your one LLM job and why a model can still be wrong. |
| 4:45–5:45 | Save, open the call log, refresh and show persisted stage/outcome. Show the lead's recent calls. Explain the amber warning for an unspecified follow-up time. |
| 5:45–6:45 | Show the live Vercel app and Render health response, then the architecture. Name Supabase's three tables and Mistral `ministral-8b-2512`. Explain where keys live and one tradeoff or next improvement. |

## Before recording

- Rehearse the flow once; keep only these prompts nearby.
- Keep your camera overlay visible without covering the controls. Confirm microphone sound and screen readability.
- Warm the Render service by opening its health endpoint.
- Use fictional demo records. Keep keys, `.env` files and account dashboards out of the recording.
- Choose a provided transcript scenario without uploading or redistributing its source file.
- Demonstrate the product as it actually works; do not claim the model guarantees correctness or that transcript analysis proves future follow-ups never happen.

## Before submitting

- Play the recording: you are visible and audible, the walkthrough works, duration is **at least 6:00**.
- Upload the video file to Google Drive. Enable **anyone with the link can view** and verify the shared link without the owner's login.
- Submit the public GitHub URL and Drive video URL in their respective task-page boxes.
- Complete the CEO WhatsApp message yourself after the task page accepts the work.
